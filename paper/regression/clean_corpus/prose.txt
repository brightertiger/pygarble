Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?
Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page and follow the email.
Can I change the name on my account?
Yes. Open Settings, choose Profile, and edit your display name.
Where can I see past invoices?
Invoices are listed under Billing with the date and amount for each one.
Is my data backed up?
Backups run every night and are kept for thirty days.
Who do I contact about access?
Ask your workspace administrator, who can add or remove members.
